# Research and Benchmark Plan

## Candidate research direction
A unified multi-cloud, temporal, identity-aware attack-path model combining configuration state, identity/resource relationships, and runtime events to assess path activation and choose minimum-impact containment. This is a hypothesis, not a novelty claim until literature review and comparisons are complete.

## Candidate algorithms
A1 multi-signal attack detection; A2 temporal attack activation; A3 dynamic attack-path risk propagation; A4 next-stage progression estimation; A5 minimum-impact path breaking.

## Evaluation
Define lawful datasets, deterministic baselines, relevant existing methods, precision/recall/false-positive rate, time-to-detect, path-risk quality, path-break success, impact cost, reversibility, and verification success. Separate offline replay from live experiments. Do not claim exact prediction or novelty without evidence.
